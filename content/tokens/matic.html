What is Polygon?

Polygon (formerly Matic Network) is a Layer 2 scaling solution for Ethereum that aims to provide faster and cheaper transactions while maintaining the security of the Ethereum mainnet. Founded in 2017 by Jaynti Kanani, Sandeep Nailwal, and Anurag Arjun, Polygon has become one of the most widely used scaling solutions.

How Polygon Works

Polygon operates as a sidechain to Ethereum, processing transactions on its own Proof of Stake network while periodically checkpointing to Ethereum for security. Users can bridge assets between Ethereum and Polygon, enjoying much lower fees while retaining EVM compatibility.

Key Features

  • EVM Compatible: Seamlessly deploy Ethereum dApps
  • Low Fees: Transactions cost fractions of a cent
  • Fast Finality: ~2 second block times
  • Ethereum Security: Checkpoints to Ethereum mainnet
  • Multi-Chain Vision: Suite of scaling solutions

Tokenomics

  • Maximum Supply: 10,000,000,000 MATIC
  • Block Time: ~2 seconds
  • Staking Rewards: Variable based on network usage
  • Use: Gas fees, staking, and governance
  • Consensus: Proof of Stake with checkpoints

Use Cases

  • Scaling Ethereum dApps
  • Gaming and metaverse platforms
  • NFT minting and trading
  • DeFi with lower transaction costs
  • Enterprise blockchain solutions

Ecosystem

Polygon hosts thousands of dApps including major DeFi protocols like Aave, Uniswap, and SushiSwap. Its zero-knowledge rollup solutions (Polygon zkEVM) represent the next evolution in Ethereum scaling, offering both security and scalability.
